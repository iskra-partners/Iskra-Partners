The challenges we address
01

Ukrainian technology companies face structural barriers to international expansion

Ukraine has developed one of Europe's most dynamic innovation ecosystems, counting thousands of companies across defense and dual-use, AI, energy, cybersecurity, cleantech and agritech. Built under exceptionally demanding conditions, these companies combine technological excellence with strong operational experience.

As they expand internationally, they navigate new regulatory environments, procurement systems, investor expectations and industrial ecosystems. Turning technological excellence into sustainable commercial growth requires access to the right partners, capital and institutional networks.

02

Ukraine's reconstruction is creating long-term opportunities for international industry

Ukraine's reconstruction needs are substantial, with large-scale financing required across critical sectors. Delivering at this scale requires strong private-sector participation, supported by a growing set of financial mechanisms designed to encourage and de-risk engagement. At the same time, the country's operating environment is evolving quickly, driven by rapid innovation, reforms aligned with EU standards and urgent on-the-ground needs.

Companies that engage early, understand the institutional landscape and establish trusted local partnerships are well positioned to develop long-term opportunities in one of Europe's largest reconstruction programs.

What we do
01

For Ukrainian tech companies scaling abroad

Defence & dual-use · Agritech · Cybersecurity · GovTech · AI

From innovative technologies to sustainable commercial growth.

  • Market strategy: define priority markets, customer segments, commercial pathways and regulatory requirements for international expansion
  • Investment & financing: identify relevant investors, public funding opportunities and strategic partners aligned with the company's growth strategy
  • Business development: facilitate access to institutional, industrial and commercial stakeholders, while supporting strategic partnerships and first market opportunities
  • International positioning: adapt commercial messaging, technical documentation and market positioning to international investors, customers and procurement environments
02

For European companies entering Ukraine

Energy · Water · Critical Infrastructure

From reconstruction priorities to financeable and implementable projects.

  • Opportunity identification: match international capabilities with priority reconstruction needs identified alongside Ukrainian public institutions and local implementation partners
  • Project preparation: define project scope, implementation arrangements, local partnerships and financing strategy, including public, private and blended finance solutions
  • Financing alignment: prepare technical and financial documentation needed to access financial instruments, in line with the requirements of international financial institutions, donors and export credit agencies
  • Implementation support: coordinate local stakeholders, institutional counterparts and project follow-up through the preparation and implementation phases
