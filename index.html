Services
01

Strategy and international business development

Helping startups, industrial and service companies assess opportunities and establish a presence in new markets.

We support companies from initial market assessment through commercial development and preparation of local operations. The scope reflects the company’s objectives, resources and requirements in the target market.

In practice
  • Market assessment and entry strategy Assessment of demand, customer needs and competition to identify priority markets and suitable entry routes. The analysis considers relevant procurement practices, regulatory requirements and potential commercial partners.
  • Commercial positioning and adaptation of the offer Refinement of the value proposition for target customers and adaptation of commercial materials to local market practices. This includes the preparation of pitch materials and investor presentations.
  • Customer prospecting and partner qualification Identification of prospective customers, distributors and industrial partners, followed by targeted outreach and qualification. We support business meetings and trade-show participation, with subsequent commercial follow-up.
  • Local establishment and cross-border structuring Comparison of options for operating locally, including distribution agreements, partnerships, subsidiaries and joint ventures. With specialist legal counsel, we clarify establishment requirements and map relevant public funding and private investment options.
02

Sector advisory and technical assistance

Expertise supporting development programmes in developing and emerging economies.

We provide energy-sector and industrial expertise to public institutions, development partners and programme implementers.

In practice
  • Policy and regulatory frameworks, energy-sector reform Sector diagnostics, regulatory gap assessments and alignment with EU frameworks, including electricity-market reform, renewable-energy permitting and energy communities. Analysis of institutional responsibilities and implementation barriers, with regulatory recommendations.
  • Private-sector engagement and project finance Private-sector engagement strategies, investor outreach and partnership facilitation. Identification and screening of investment opportunities, with assessment of financing mechanisms and risk-mitigation instruments.
  • Capacity building and knowledge transfer Training needs assessments and development of tailored learning materials. Applied training on energy reform, industrial decarbonisation and project preparation.
  • Energy planning and industrial decarbonisation Energy planning, scenario analysis and investment prioritisation. Assessment of renewable-energy, storage and flexibility options, industrial decarbonisation roadmaps and CBAM assessments.
03

Ukraine reconstruction: energy and water sectors

We prepare projects with Ukrainian beneficiaries, international companies and specialist partners.

Our work connects priority reconstruction needs with relevant industrial capabilities and potential financing mechanisms. We support the preparation of project scope and delivery arrangements, coordinating the institutional, technical, legal and financial contributions required.

In practice
  • Identify needs and assess project readiness Collaboration with municipal and regional authorities to identify priority recovery needs. Preliminary assessment of project maturity, beneficiary commitment and institutional responsibilities.
  • Define scope and coordinate technical contributions Identification and mobilisation of companies and specialist partners with technologies suited to the identified needs. Definition of project scope, expected results and partner responsibilities.
  • Assess financing and prepare funding applications Assessment of financing eligibility and relevant grants, concessional finance, guarantees and risk-mitigation instruments. Preparation of project concepts and funding applications aligned with financing institutions’ requirements.
  • Support implementation, coordination and reporting Facilitation of coordination among beneficiaries, public authorities, participating companies and technical partners. Monitoring of agreed workplans and milestones, with consolidation of progress and results reporting.
