The challenges we address

Ukrainian tech companies face structural barriers when entering Western markets

Ukrainian technologies are proven and already deployed in real-world conditions across strategic sectors such as defense, energy, cyber and critical infrastructure. The ecosystem now counts more than 2,600 active startups, built global-first by design and optimized for speed, constraints and cost-efficiency. As these companies expand internationally, success depends on aligning strong technologies with local procurement norms, compliance frameworks and industrial ecosystems. Entering European and US markets typically means navigating certification paths, long procurement cycles and fragmented institutional buyers, while securing the right capital, partners and regulatory pathways for long-term deployment.

Ukraine's reconstruction is creating major opportunities for European companies

Ukraine's reconstruction needs are substantial, with large-scale financing requirements across critical sectors. Delivering at this scale requires strong private-sector participation, supported by a growing set of financial mechanisms designed to encourage and de-risk engagement. At the same time, the country's operating environment is evolving quickly, driven by rapid innovation, reforms aligned with EU standards and urgent on-the-ground needs. Companies that engage early, with solid local anchoring and access to public, private and blended finance, can build strong positioning, develop hard-to-replicate relationships and turn early projects into long-term opportunities.

On both sides, the bottleneck is the same: turning opportunities into structured, bankable and deployable projects. This is the gap ISKRA operates.

What we do

For Ukrainian tech companies scaling abroad

  • Structure market entry by defining priority geographies, target buyers and sequencing regulatory, industrial and commercial steps, including compliance with export control frameworks and dual-use regulations relevant to strategic sectors
  • Design and run financing strategies combining private capital, public instruments and strategic partnerships, including access to European and US institutional mechanisms supporting technology deployment
  • Secure institutional positioning, build industrial and commercial anchoring, and enable first deployments within EU and US markets

For European companies entering Ukraine

  • Identify opportunities on the ground, aligned with Ukraine's reconstruction needs and priorities
  • Structure projects (pilot deployments, feasibility studies, reconstruction projects implementation) by defining scope, delivery partners, and a funding approach (including public, private and blended options such as risk-sharing instruments, export credit solutions and grant-backed pilots) across relevant sectors
  • Prepare the technical and financial documentation required for applying for relevant financial mechanisms and stay engaged through implementation, supporting coordination until projects are effectively delivered
We provide the structured execution required to move from opportunity to funded project and from strategy to real deployment.
