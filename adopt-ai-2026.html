The summit

Adopt AI 2026

Adopt AI is the business continuation of the AI Action Summit, organised by Artefact at the Grand Palais. President Emmanuel Macron spoke at the 2025 edition, which drew more than 20,000 people and over 500 speakers.

The programme is organised by sector: Health, Finance, Industry, Retail, Travel, People, Society, Planet, Sport, and Defense.

Why attend

Who you meet

European defence and aerospace groups and large industrials, the startups presenting on the Startup Stage, and French and European investors across both days. Adopt AI runs a Defense track alongside its other sectors.

The Defense track →    Published speakers →

What it is worth to a Ukrainian company

European industrial groups are buying and partnering in defence technology, dual-use systems and applied AI. The summit puts those buyers, investors and potential partners in one building for two days.

Who should come

Ukrainian startups and scale-ups in defence technology, dual-use technologies and applied artificial intelligence, with a concrete interest in European customers, partners or capital.

Our role

We bring the delegation together and handle the relationship with Artefact: the programme, the practical arrangements and your questions before and during the summit.

About ISKRA Partners

ISKRA Partners is a Paris-based advisory firm that connects Ukrainian technology companies with European and US markets, and European industrial players with reconstruction projects in Ukraine.

For Adopt AI 2026 we bring the Ukrainian delegation together with Kharkiv IT Cluster, and act as the single point of contact with Artefact, from the programme to logistics.
